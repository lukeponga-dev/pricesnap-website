GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

A little context before your first snap.

What is PriceSnap?

PriceSnap is an Android app for AI-assisted resale valuations, with a focus on New Zealand. It helps identify an item from a photo and provides context for an estimated resale value in NZD.

Is the valuation a guaranteed selling price?

No. It is an estimate to help you choose a starting price. Actual sale prices depend on condition, demand, location, timing and how you sell. Verify the item details and compare current market evidence.

What does the confidence score mean?

It provides a signal of uncertainty around the estimate. It is not an accuracy guarantee or the probability that your item will sell. Review the supporting details, especially when evidence is limited.

What makes a good photo?

Use good light and a plain background. Keep the whole item visible and capture the brand or model label and signs of wear. Avoid including personal information in your photos.

Does PriceSnap buy or sell my items?

PriceSnap helps you research a resale price. You choose where to list the item and agree a final price with a buyer.

Where can I check pricing and device compatibility?

The Google Play listing has the current availability, pricing and Android compatibility details. View PriceSnap on Google Play.

Where can I find privacy and data deletion information?

Open the app’s Google Play listing and review its Data safety, privacy policy and developer contact information for the current data-handling and deletion options.
