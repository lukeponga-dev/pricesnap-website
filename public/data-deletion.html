Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like to delete your data associated with PriceSnap, please follow the instructions below based on the type of data you wish to remove.

1. Local App Data (Scan History & Cache)

Scan history, cached thumbnails, and appraisal results are stored locally on your Android device.

  • Delete specific history: Open the PriceSnap app, go to Settings, and select Delete scan history.
  • Remove all local data: You can clear all data by going to your Android device's Settings → Apps → PriceSnap → Storage → Clear storage (or "Clear data"), or by simply uninstalling the PriceSnap app.

2. Account Data

If you have signed into PriceSnap, your account data (email address, user identifier) is stored securely via Firebase Authentication.

  • To delete your PriceSnap account, please use the Delete account option located within the app's settings.
  • If you are unable to access the app, you may request account deletion by emailing us at developmentdesignsltd@gmail.com.

Note: Account deletion removes your account from PriceSnap's Firebase Authentication. This does not automatically delete information retained independently by other service providers (such as Google or Vercel logs) as permitted by their own policies.
